Part I

Privacy Policy

The short version: We use only the information needed to deliver personal-assistant reminders, status updates, and requested notifications. We do not use your mobile information for third-party marketing.

Information we collect

We may collect information you provide directly, including your name, mobile number, message content, communication preferences, and details needed to provide a reminder, update, or notification you requested. We may also receive basic delivery information from our communications provider, such as whether a message was delivered or failed.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive information unless you choose to include it in a request. Please avoid sending information that is not needed for the service.

How we use information

We use information to:

  • send reminders, status updates, service notices, and other messages you request;
  • respond to questions and support requests;
  • honor opt-in and opt-out choices;
  • maintain message delivery, prevent abuse, and meet legal or carrier requirements.

We do not use SMS consent data or mobile numbers for unrelated advertising.

How we share information

We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not share mobile numbers, text-message opt-in data, or consent records with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Mobile information and messaging consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are collected only to operate this messaging service and are never disclosed for third-party marketing.

We may share limited information with service providers that help deliver the requested service, such as telecommunications and messaging providers. They may use that information only to perform services for us and must handle it under their own privacy and security obligations. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or safety, or in connection with a lawful business transfer.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except service providers that enable the messaging service.

Retention and security

We keep personal information only as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, maintain required consent or opt-out records, resolve disputes, and comply with law. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no electronic system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your privacy rights

You may ask to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, subject to legal exceptions. You may also withdraw SMS consent at any time as described below.

Children’s privacy

The service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Policy changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the service or legal requirements. The effective date above will be updated when a change takes effect.

Messaging Terms & Conditions

The short version: Messages are limited to the personal-assistant updates you agreed to receive. Frequency varies. Standard message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to end messages or HELP for help.

Consent and program description

By providing your mobile number and asking to receive messages, you consent to receive recurring SMS or MMS messages from Wally about requested personal-assistant services, including appointment reminders, status updates, and notifications. Consent may be provided verbally or in writing and is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

Message frequency and charges

Message frequency varies based on the reminders, updates, and notifications you request. Message and data rates may apply according to your wireless plan. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Opt out and help

  • Reply STOP to any message to opt out. You may receive one final confirmation message.
  • Reply START to opt back in after opting out.
  • Reply HELP for help, or email wally.dk24@gmail.com.

Opt-out requests are honored for the mobile number that sent the request. If your number changes, please contact us so we can update our records.

You agree to provide an accurate mobile number that you control and to use the service only for lawful purposes. Do not use the service to send harmful, deceptive, abusive, or unlawful content.

The service is provided on an “as available” basis. We may modify, suspend, or discontinue messaging when necessary for security, carrier compliance, legal requirements, or service availability. To the extent permitted by law, the service is provided without warranties, and we are not responsible for indirect or consequential losses arising from message delays, delivery failures, or reliance on a notification.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms as the messaging service or applicable requirements change. Continued use after updated terms take effect means you accept the revised terms. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP.
